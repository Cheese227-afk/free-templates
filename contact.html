Contact us

We would love to hear from you. Whether you have a question about using Starter Pages, found something that does not work, or have an idea for a new template, send us a message and we will get back to you as soon as we can.

Email

Write to us at freetemplate384@gmail.com. We aim to reply within a few business days.

What to include

  • For a bug report: the template you were using, your browser and device, and what you expected to happen compared with what happened. Screenshots help a lot.
  • For a question about the downloaded file: tell us where you are hosting it and what you are trying to change.
  • For a template request: describe the kind of site you want to make and which sections it needs.
  • For privacy requests: mention the page you are writing about so we can respond accurately. See our privacy policy for how data is handled.

Before you write

The frequently asked questions on the home page answer the most common questions about pricing, image handling, commercial use and editing the downloaded file. Your answer may already be there.

Please note that Starter Pages is a tool and does not host or manage your website, so we cannot help with problems on your own hosting account or domain. Your hosting provider's support team is the best place for those.
