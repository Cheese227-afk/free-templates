About Starter Pages

Starter Pages is a free online tool that helps people build a simple website without writing code. You choose a template, enter your name, tagline and email, pick colors and a font style, add your own images, and download a single HTML file that is ready to upload to your web host.

Why we built it

Many small businesses, freelancers, students, clubs and community groups only need a clean one-page website. They need to say who they are, show a few photos and give people a way to get in touch. Full website builders can feel heavy and expensive for that job, and starting from an empty text file is intimidating. Starter Pages sits in the middle: a few well-designed layouts that you can personalize in a couple of minutes and then own completely.

What you can make

  • Business landing page with a headline, three benefit cards, an optional photo gallery and a contact button.
  • Portfolio with an introduction and a grid of your projects.
  • Blog with a header and a list of recent posts that you can duplicate and edit.
  • Restaurant menu with a hero banner, a dish list with prices, photos and opening hours.

How it works

Everything happens in your browser. The live preview is generated on your device, your images are resized on your device, and the file you download is assembled on your device. We do not receive your text or your pictures. Because the result is plain HTML and CSS with no external dependencies, you can host it almost anywhere, edit it in any text editor, and hand it to a developer later if you want to extend it.

Our principles

  • Simple. A small set of controls that do what they say.
  • Yours. The files you download belong to you. There is no lock-in and no watermark.
  • Private. No account is needed and your content stays on your device. Read the privacy policy for the full details.
  • Accessible. The templates use readable type, sensible contrast and layouts that adapt to small screens.
