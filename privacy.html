Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

This policy explains what information Starter Pages ("we", "us") handles when you visit this website and use the template tool, and what choices you have.

Content you enter in the tool

The text, colors, logo and photos you enter into the template tool are processed entirely in your web browser. They are used to build the live preview and the HTML file you download. This content is not uploaded to or stored on our servers, and we cannot see it. Once you close or reload the page, it is gone unless you saved the downloaded file.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, our hosting provider may keep standard server logs that include your IP address, browser type, the pages requested and the date and time of the request. These logs are used for security, troubleshooting and basic traffic statistics.

Cookies and advertising

We use Google AdSense, a service provided by Google, to show or to prepare to show advertisements on this website. Google and its partners may use cookies and similar technologies, including advertising identifiers, to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites, to measure ad performance and to prevent fraud and abuse.

You can learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. You can manage personalized advertising in your Google account's ad settings at adssettings.google.com, and you can opt out of personalized ads from many other providers at aboutads.info. You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings, although parts of some websites may not work as expected if you do.

Where required by law, such as in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, advertising cookies and similar technologies are used only with your consent.

How we use information

We use the limited information described above to operate and secure the website, understand how it is used so that we can improve it, respond to messages you send us, and comply with legal obligations. We do not sell personal information.

Messages you send us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message. We use it only to reply and to improve the service, and we keep it only as long as needed for those purposes.

Third-party links and services

This site loads fonts from Google Fonts and may link to other websites. Those services have their own privacy practices, and we are not responsible for them.

Children

Starter Pages is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask what personal information we hold about you, to request correction or deletion, or to object to certain processing. To make a request, please use the details on our contact page.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed. Continued use of the site after an update means you accept the revised policy.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, please reach out through our contact page.
